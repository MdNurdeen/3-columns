# DollarDesi: TFSA 2027 (new room) script

**Length:** about 75 seconds · **Style:** Hinglish, fast and friendly
**When to post:** the day the CRA announces the 2027 TFSA limit (expected November 2026)

Numbers are written as words so ElevenLabs reads them clearly.

---

**[HOOK]**
Ruko! Naya saal aane wala hai… aur tumhare TFSA ka room badhne wala hai. Kitna? Aur kya tum isse sahi use kar rahe ho?

**[NEW LIMIT]**
Do hazaar chhabbis mein TFSA limit thi saat hazaar dollar. Do hazaar sattaais ke liye limit hai… saat hazaar paanch sau dollar. Yaani poore paanch sau dollar zyada room!

**[TOTAL ROOM]**
Aur agar tum do hazaar nau se Canada mein ho, aur tab athaarah saal ke the… toh tumhara total room ho jayega lagbhag ek lakh solah hazaar paanch sau dollar. Socho!

**[NEWCOMERS]**
Newcomer ho? Suno. Tumhara room us saal se shuru hota hai jab tum Canada ke resident bane, aur athaarah saal ke ho. Pehle ke saalon ka room nahi milta. Lekin jis saal aaye, us saal ka poora room milta hai.

**[CARRY FORWARD]**
Is saal ka room use nahi kiya? Tension mat lo. Woh khatam nahi hota, aage carry forward hota rehta hai.

**[THE TRAP]**
Lekin ek galti mat karna. Is saal paise nikaale, toh woh room agle saal ek January ko wapas milta hai. Usi saal wapas daal diya… toh over-contribution! Aur har mahine ek percent penalty.

**[WHAT TO DO]**
Toh karna kya hai? Ek: CRA My Account mein apna asli room check karo. Do: January mein naya room aate hi plan banao. Teen: dabbe ko sirf cash se mat bharo. Lambi race ke liye diversified, low-cost investments.

**[CTA]**
Yeh video us dost ko bhejo jiska TFSA khaali pada hai! Yeh education hai, financial advice nahi. Follow karo DollarDesi!
