# DollarDesi: Year-end tax moves before Dec 31 (script)

**Length:** about 85 seconds · **Style:** Hinglish, fast and friendly
**When to post:** early to mid December 2026 (people search this most in the last 3 weeks of the year)

Numbers are written as words so ElevenLabs reads them clearly.

---

**[HOOK]**
Ruko! Ikattees December ke baad yeh paisa wapas nahi aayega! Paanch tax moves jo saal khatam hone se pehle karne hain. Last wala sabse zyada log galat samajhte hain!

**[MOVE 1: FHSA]**
Number ek: FHSA. Pehla ghar lene ka plan hai? FHSA khol lo, ikattees December se pehle. Kyunki room tabhi se shuru hota hai jab account khulta hai. Har saal aath hazaar dollar, aur lifetime chaalees hazaar. Aur jo daaloge, woh tax se minus. RRSP jaisa refund, TFSA jaisa tax-free!

**[MOVE 2: RESP]**
Number do: bacche hain? RESP mein pachchees sau dollar daalo, aur government deti hai bees percent, yaani paanch sau dollar free! Har bacche ke liye, har saal. Yeh free paisa chhodna mat.

**[MOVE 3: DONATIONS]**
Number teen: donation. Mandir, masjid, gurudwara ya charity: registered charity ko diya, toh receipt lo. Ikattees December tak ka donation is saal ke tax mein count hota hai. Aur pati-patni dono ki receipts ek saath claim kar sakte ho, toh credit zyada milta hai.

**[MOVE 4: TFSA TIMING]**
Number chaar: TFSA se paise nikaalne hain? Toh December mein nikaalo, January mein nahi. Kyunki room agle saal ek January ko wapas aata hai. December mein nikaala, toh room ek saal jaldi wapas!

**[MOVE 5: RRSP MYTH]**
Aur number paanch, sabse bada myth: RRSP ka deadline ikattees December nahi hai! Do hazaar chhabbis ke liye last date hai ek March, do hazaar sattaais. Toh December mein ghabrao mat. Lekin FHSA aur RESP ka deadline ikattees December hi hai. Yeh mix mat karna!

**[RECAP + CTA]**
Toh list yaad rakho: FHSA kholo, RESP grant lo, donation receipt lo, TFSA December mein nikaalo, aur RRSP ke liye March tak time hai. Yeh video save karo, aur us dost ko bhejo jo har saal last minute pe yaad karta hai! Yeh education hai, financial advice nahi. Follow karo DollarDesi!

## Recording tips

- Read numbers exactly as written in words.
- Pause briefly before each "Number ek / do / teen…": that's where the scenes change.
- Leave half a second of silence after "Follow karo DollarDesi!"
