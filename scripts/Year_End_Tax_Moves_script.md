# DollarDesi: Year-end tax moves before Dec 31

**Length:** about 85–90 seconds · **Post:** early to mid December 2026
**Numbers verified:** October 30, 2026 (sources at the bottom)

---

## 1. Script to paste into ElevenLabs

Paste one section at a time, using the same voice and settings each time. "…" means a short pause.

```
Ruko! Ikattees December ke baad… yeh paisa wapas nahi aayega! Paanch tax moves, jo saal khatam hone se pehle karne hain. Aur last wala… sabse zyada log galat samajhte hain!

Number ek: FHSA. Pehla ghar lene ka plan hai? Toh FHSA khol lo, ikattees December se pehle. Kyunki room tabhi shuru hota hai, jab account khulta hai. Har saal aath hazaar dollar, aur lifetime chaalees hazaar. Aur jo daaloge, woh tax se minus. RRSP jaisa refund… TFSA jaisa tax-free!

Number do: bacche hain? RESP mein pachchees sau dollar daalo… aur government deti hai bees percent. Yaani paanch sau dollar, bilkul free! Har bacche ke liye, har saal. Yeh free paisa… chhodna mat.

Number teen: donation. Mandir, masjid, gurudwara, ya koi charity. Registered charity ko diya, toh official receipt zaroor lo. Ikattees December tak charity tak pahuncha donation, isi saal ke tax mein count hota hai. Aur pati-patni dono ki receipts, ek hi return mein claim kar sakte ho. Credit zyada milta hai!

Number chaar: TFSA se paise nikaalne hain? Toh December mein nikaalo… January mein nahi. Kyunki room, agle saal ek January ko wapas aata hai. December mein nikaala, toh room… ek saal jaldi wapas!

Aur number paanch, sabse bada myth: RRSP ka deadline ikattees December nahi hai! Do hazaar chhabbis ke liye, last date hai ek March, do hazaar sattaais. Lekin FHSA aur RESP ka deadline? Ikattees December hi hai. Inhe mix mat karna!

Aur ek tip: aakhri din ka wait mat karo. Bank transfer mein din lag jaate hain.

Toh list yaad rakho: FHSA kholo. RESP grant lo. Donation receipt lo. TFSA December mein nikaalo. Aur RRSP ke liye, March tak time hai. Yeh video save karo, aur us dost ko bhejo… jo har saal last minute pe yaad karta hai! Yeh education hai, financial advice nahi. Follow karo DollarDesi!
```

## 2. How to speak it: rhythm and style

**Overall feel:** a smart desi friend giving a warning over chai. Excited and a little urgent, never like a news anchor. Smile while you talk; it comes through in the voice.

**Pace:** brisk, about 150–160 words per minute. Slightly faster in the lists, slower on numbers.

| Section | Tone | Speed | Stress these words | Pause |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | urgent, wide-eyed warning | fast | **Ruko!**, **wapas nahi**, **galat** | full stop after "Ruko!"; short pause before "sabse zyada" |
| **1. FHSA** | helpful, "suno yeh kaam ki baat hai" | medium | **khol lo**, **aath hazaar**, **chaalees hazaar**, **refund… tax-free** | short pause before "TFSA jaisa" (it's the punchline) |
| **2. RESP** | excited, as if giving away free money | medium-fast | **bees percent**, **paanch sau**, **free** | pause before "chhodna mat" |
| **3. Donation** | warm, respectful (it's about mandir/masjid/gurudwara) | medium | **official receipt**, **ikattees December**, **pati-patni** | none |
| **4. TFSA** | clever, "trick batata hoon" | medium | **December**, **January nahi**, **ek saal jaldi** | pause before "ek saal jaldi" |
| **5. RRSP myth** | myth-buster, slightly dramatic | slower on the date | **nahi hai!**, **ek March**, **ikattees December hi** | pause after "deadline?" |
| **Tip** | quick, friendly aside | fast, a bit softer | **wait mat karo** | none |
| **Recap + CTA** | upbeat, rhythmic list | each item crisp, like ticking boxes | the 5 verbs: **kholo, lo, lo, nikaalo, time hai** | tiny beat between list items; the disclaimer is calmer; end bright on **Follow karo DollarDesi!** |

**Numbers:** say them slowly and clearly, one beat slower than the rest of the sentence. They're what people screenshot.

**Do:**
- Keep the **same energy and mic distance** from start to end. The hook shouldn't be louder than the rest; that caused the volume drop in the paycheque video.
- Leave a **half-second pause** between sections (that's where the scenes change) and half a second of silence at the very end.

**Don't:**
- Rush the numbers.
- Whisper the disclaimer. Calm is fine, but it should still be clear.

### ElevenLabs settings (a starting point; adjust by ear)

| Setting | Suggested | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Voice | your usual DollarDesi voice | consistency across videos |
| Stability | **40–50%** | lower = more expressive; above 60% sounds flat |
| Similarity | **75–80%** | keeps it sounding like your voice |
| Style exaggeration | **15–25%** | adds energy; too high gets shaky |
| Speaker boost | **On** | clearer voice |
| Speed | **1.0–1.05×** | brisk but clear |

Generate each section separately with the **same settings**. If one sounds off, regenerate only that one. Then join them, or send me all the clips and I'll join them.

## 3. Numbers: verified

| On screen | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| FHSA annual limit | $8,000 | ✅ |
| FHSA lifetime limit | $40,000 | ✅ |
| FHSA room starts only after you open the first FHSA | — | ✅ |
| FHSA deadline | **Dec 31**, with **no** 60-day grace period (unlike RRSP) | ✅ CRA |
| RESP grant (CESG) | 20% on the first $2,500 = **$500 per child per year** (lifetime max $7,200) | ✅ Government of Canada |
| Donations | count if the charity **receives** them by Dec 31 (mail goes by postmark) | ✅ CRA |
| Spouses can pool donation receipts on one return | — | ✅ |
| TFSA withdrawal room | returns Jan 1 of the next year | ✅ |
| RRSP deadline for the 2026 tax year | **March 1, 2027** | ✅ |

Not in the script on purpose, to keep it short and safe: exact donation credit rates (the federal rate on the first $200 changed with the 2025–26 tax cut, so it's easy to get wrong), the lower-income RESP top-up, and FHSA carry-forward.
