# DollarDesi: RRSP 2027 script

**Length:** about 80 seconds · **Style:** Hinglish, fast and friendly
**When to post:** now through February (RRSP season peaks before the deadline)

Numbers are written as words so ElevenLabs reads them clearly.

---

**[HOOK]**
Canada aate hi RRSP mein paisa daal diya? Ruko! Pehle saal tumhara RRSP room zero bhi ho sakta hai!

**[WHAT IS RRSP]**
RRSP matlab Registered Retirement Savings Plan. Isme jo paisa daaloge, woh tumhari income se minus hota hai. Yaani tax kam, aur refund zyada!

**[EXAMPLE]**
Socho tumne paanch hazaar dollar daale, aur tumhara tax rate lagbhag tees percent hai. Toh refund aa sakta hai lagbhag pandrah sau dollar. Seedha tumhari jeb mein!

**[2027 LIMIT]**
Do hazaar sattaais ki maximum limit hai paintees hazaar teen sau nabbe dollar. Lekin dhyaan do: tumhara room hota hai pichle saal ki earned income ka athaarah percent. Maximum tak pahunchne ke liye income chahiye lagbhag ek lakh chhiyaanve hazaar dollar.

**[NEWCOMERS]**
Aur yahi newcomers ki sabse badi galti hai. RRSP room pichle saal ki Canadian income se banta hai. Is saal aaye ho? Toh room agle saal milega. Apna asli room CRA My Account ya Notice of Assessment mein check karo.

**[THE TRAP]**
Aur ek trap. TFSA se paise nikaalo toh room wapas milta hai. RRSP se nikaalo toh… room hamesha ke liye gaya! Upar se woh paisa income ban jaata hai, aur tax katta hai.

**[FIRST HOME BONUS]**
Lekin ek achhi baat. Pehla ghar le rahe ho? Home Buyers' Plan se RRSP se saath hazaar dollar tak nikaal sakte ho, aur pandrah saal mein wapas daalna hota hai.

**[RRSP OR TFSA]**
Toh RRSP ya TFSA? Simple rule: income zyada hai aur tax rate high hai, toh RRSP. Income kam hai, toh pehle TFSA.

**[DEADLINE + CTA]**
Do hazaar chhabbis ke tax ke liye last date hai ek March, do hazaar sattaais. Yeh video us dost ko bhejo jo har saal refund miss karta hai! Yeh education hai, financial advice nahi. Follow karo DollarDesi!
