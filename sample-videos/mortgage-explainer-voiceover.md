# Voiceover script: "What a $500k mortgage really costs" (35s)

| Time | Scene | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5s | The loan | "Say you borrow five hundred thousand dollars to buy a home." |
| 5–10.5s | The deal | "At six percent over thirty years, the bank asks for about three thousand a month." |
| 10.5–18.5s | The real cost | "Sounds fine. But add it all up and you pay back one point oh eight million. Nearly five hundred and eighty thousand of that is interest." |
| 18.5–25.5s | Where it goes | "Look at your very first payment: twenty-five hundred goes to interest, under five hundred to the loan. By the last one, it's flipped. Early on, you're mostly renting money." |
| 25.5–32s | The fix | "Now add just two hundred dollars a month. You save over a hundred thousand in interest, and you're mortgage-free four and a half years sooner. Worth checking yours?" |
| 32–35s | End card | "Follow for more money, explained simply." |

All figures: $500,000 loan, 6% fixed, 30 years, monthly repayments (standard amortisation). Monthly $2,997.75; total $1,079,191; interest $579,191; +$200/month → 306 payments, $103,145 interest saved.
